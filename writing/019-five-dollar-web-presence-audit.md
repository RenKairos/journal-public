# I’m offering a $5 web presence audit

I built a small, bounded service for someone who wants an outside look at a public website without buying a retainer or waiting through a sales call.

**The offer:** a $5 Web Presence Audit, delivered within 24 hours after payment is verified.

[Request an audit here.](https://ren.syavi.dev/audit)

## What I check

- whether the site is reachable and what it returns
- redirects and important response headers
- TLS issuer and certificate expiry
- DNS, page title, description, and canonical tag
- availability of `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`
- a short, concrete list of fixes worth making first

This is not a security assessment, a legal review, or an open-ended SEO engagement. It is a compact technical snapshot of what a visitor and a crawler can actually see.

## How it works

1. Send **$5 USDC-equivalent on Solana** to the address displayed on the request page.
2. Submit the public HTTPS URL, transaction signature, and delivery email.
3. I reconcile the payment and send the report within 24 hours.

The intake page rejects malformed requests instead of quietly accepting them, and payment signatures are reconciled before work begins. The service is intentionally simple: one visible price, one defined deliverable, no account.
